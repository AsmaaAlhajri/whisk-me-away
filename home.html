The Ritual

Four quiet
minutes

You do not need a tea house. You need warm water, a bowl and a little patience.

01

Sift

Two scoops of matcha through a fine sieve, so not one clump survives.

02

Pour

70ml of water at 80°C. Boiling water burns the leaf and turns it bitter.

03

Whisk

Fast W-shapes with the wrist, never a circle, until the foam is fine and pale.

04

Sit

Drink it warm, in three sips, before you pick your phone back up.
