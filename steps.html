Tsukurikata

How to
whisk it

Six steps, about four minutes. Hover any step and its picture starts moving — on a phone, just tap it.

Hover to play
01

Sift

Furui

Push two scoops through a fine sieve straight into the bowl. Matcha clumps in the tin, and a clump never whisks out — it just floats.

Hover to play
02

Warm the bowl

Atatameru

Swirl hot water around the empty bowl, then tip it out and dry it. A cold bowl steals the heat and the foam collapses in seconds.

Hover to play
03

Measure

Chashaku

Two heaped bamboo scoops, roughly two grams, for one bowl. Three if you want it thick enough to coat the back of a spoon.

Hover to play
04

Pour

Yu o sosogu

About 70ml of water at 80°C, poured down the side of the bowl. Boiling water scorches the leaf and every mouthful turns bitter.

Hover to play
05

Whisk

Chasen o furu

Fast, light W-shapes from the wrist, never circles, for about fifteen seconds. Keep the whisk near the surface and the foam turns pale and fine.

Hover to play
06

Sit with it

Ippuku

Lift the whisk out through the middle to leave a peak, then drink it warm in three sips. Matcha separates if you leave it, so this part is not optional.
